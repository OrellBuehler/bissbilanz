Bissbilanz is a fast, no-nonsense calorie and macro tracker. Log what you eat in seconds, see your day at a glance, and keep your data in your hands.

TRACK WHAT MATTERS
• Calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber for every meal
• 43 extended nutrients, from vitamins to minerals
• Daily goals with clear progress at a glance

LOG FAST
• Scan any barcode — nutrition data comes straight from Open Food Facts
• Point the camera at a nutrition label and let on-device OCR fill in the values
• Favorites, recent foods and home-screen widgets for one-tap logging

MORE THAN FOOD
• Build recipes with automatic per-serving nutrition
• Track weight, sleep and supplements alongside your food log
• Maintenance-calorie estimate from your weight trend and food log

YOUR DATA, YOUR PACE
• Use it without an account — everything stays on your device in local-only mode
• Or sign in to sync across the web app, Android and iPhone
• Works offline; changes sync when you're back online
• No ads, no tracking SDKs, no selling data — see the privacy policy

Bissbilanz is a personal project, built for people who already know why they track and just want a tool that gets out of the way.
