Bissbilanz is a calorie and macro tracker that feels like a carefully built personal tool: fast to log, honest about your data, and fully usable without a connection.

Log a meal in seconds, see exactly where your day stands, and keep your whole history in your own hands.

WHAT YOU CAN TRACK
• Calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber — against daily goals you set yourself
• 43 extended nutrients, from vitamins and minerals to sugar, sodium and omega-3
• Body weight, with a smoothed trend instead of daily noise
• Sleep duration and sleep quality
• Daily supplements
• Fasting days

LOG FAST
• Barcode scanner — point the camera at a product and load nutrition values from Open Food Facts
• Nutrition label scan — no barcode? Point the camera at the label and the values are read right on your device
• Your own food database, favorites and recently used entries — repeat meals with one tap
• Recipe builder — combine ingredients once, then log a serving with automatically calculated nutrition
• Home-screen widgets and a Wear OS app for logging with one tap
• AI-assisted logging — connect Claude or another MCP client to your diary and just say what you ate: foods are found, portions estimated and logged for you

INSIGHTS THAT ACTUALLY HELP
• Weekly and monthly statistics
• An estimate of your maintenance calories from your weight trend and what you actually ate
• Streaks, meal timing and food diversity
• A breakdown of every nutrient per meal and per day

WORKS OFFLINE
Every device stores your diary locally and syncs conflict-free once you're back online. Log on a plane, in a basement gym, or anywhere without reception.

HEALTH CONNECT
Share nutrition, weight and sleep with Health Connect — permission by permission, only if you want to. Weight and sleep can also be read in, so your scale or sleep tracker keeps your diary current.

USABLE WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
Want to keep everything on the device? Start in local mode: no account, and your diary never touches our server. If you sign in later, your local data moves into your account.

ONLY FOR YOU
• Free, no ads and no purchases
• No tracking SDKs, no data selling
• Delete your account and all your data anytime, right in the app
• The full source code is public

There's also a web app, so the same diary is available in any browser.
